## Known gaps: don't log these as new bugs

- **Payday required at organisation setup:** iOS has no organisation setup screen (organisations are created on web).
- **Payday banner on Home:** web shows it in "Waiting for you"; iOS Home has no such card. Waiting on an owner decision about where it goes.
- **Quick add and the inline "Save booking" row in Book labour:** no rate control. Bookings follow the organisation rate.
- **Timesheet review screens:** show pay without a pay check (existing behaviour).
- **Other screens that show roster pay** (e.g. Operatives): do not call the pay rule yet.
